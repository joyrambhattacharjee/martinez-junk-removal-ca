What Junk Removal Actually Costs in Martinez, CA

Volume pricing explained without the vagueness: what an eighth of a truck holds, what pushes a quote up, and the four surcharges to ask about before anyone loads anything.

By Martinez Junk Removal

Most junk removal in Contra Costa County is priced by the space your load takes up in the truck, not by the hour and not by the pound. That sounds simple until you try to picture what a quarter of a truck looks like. Here is the arithmetic we use, the numbers we quote in 2026, and the four things that legitimately move a price.

The unit is truck volume, measured in eighths

Our dump beds hold 15 cubic yards. That is roughly the volume of a standard one-car garage stacked chest high, or about six pickup truck loads. Because a bed is a fixed box, it is easy to mark it in eighths and price each mark — which is why every quote we give references a fraction of a truck rather than an hourly rate.

Those ranges include labor, hauling and disposal fees in one number. A quote that separates the dump fee out and adds it later is not cheaper, it is just quoted differently — always ask whether disposal is included before you compare two prices.

Why volume beats hourly for you

Hourly pricing transfers every risk to the customer. If the stairs are narrow, if the crew is slow, if the truck has to shuffle a load, you pay for it. Volume pricing puts that risk on us: we look at the pile, name a number, and if the job takes twice as long as we guessed, the price does not change. That is also why we ask for photos before quoting instead of giving a number sight unseen.

The four things that genuinely change a quote

  1. Weight-dense material. Concrete, dirt, sod, roofing shingles, tile and plaster hit the truck's legal weight limit long before they fill the bed. These are quoted by the cubic yard, and any honest hauler will tell you the same. Ask up front rather than being surprised.
  2. Access and stairs. A driveway drop is not the same job as a third-floor walk-up with a switchback stair, or a crawlspace you enter through a hatch. Labor scales with carrying distance, not pile size.
  3. Items with their own disposal fees. Mattresses, tires, refrigerant-bearing appliances, CRT televisions and treated lumber each carry a facility-specific charge in California. We list them individually so you can see what is a fee and what is our labor.
  4. Hazardous material we cannot take. Paint, solvents, pesticides, motor oil, propane tanks and asbestos are not truck items anywhere in California. They go to a household hazardous waste facility — see what we cannot take for the full list and where each one goes.

A firm quote should be one number, confirmed before anything is loaded, with disposal inside it. If a hauler will only give you a range on site, or the number moves once the truck is full, that is a pricing model problem — not a you problem.

What a Martinez job typically comes to

For homeowners, the two most common calls are a garage and a single-room clearout. A cluttered two-car garage in Martinez, Pleasant Hill or Concord usually lands between three-quarters and a full truck — call it $610 to $995 depending on how much shelving and lumber is in there. A single bedroom or a furniture-only pickup is more often a quarter to a half truck. See garage cleanout and furniture removal for what each includes.

For businesses, the driver is usually square footage rather than clutter. A 2,000 sq ft office decommission with cubicles and files runs one to two full trucks; a tenant turnover on a one-bedroom rental is typically a half to three-quarter load. Contractors doing phase clean-ups often find a dump trailer rental cheaper than repeat pickups, because their own crew is already on site and can load between trades.

How to get an accurate number in one phone call

  • Stand back and photograph the whole pile, not close-ups. We need the footprint and the height.
  • Say whether it is on a ground floor, up stairs, or behind a gate we need a code for.
  • Call out anything heavy — dirt, concrete, tile, roofing — separately.
  • Mention appliances, mattresses and TVs by count, since those carry set fees.
  • Tell us your city. Martinez, Pleasant Hill and Concord are same-day territory; Brentwood and Vacaville are usually next-day.

Text the photos to (925) 555-0142 and you will get a firm written number back, usually within the hour during business hours. If the pile turns out to be larger than the photos showed, we tell you before we start loading, not after.
