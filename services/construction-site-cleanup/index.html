Construction Site Clean-Up for Martinez & Bay Area Contractors

Phase clean-ups between trades, final punch-list hauls, and debris removal on demand. You call, we clear, the next crew gets a site they can actually work in — no dumpster sitting on the lot collecting the neighbours' furniture.

  • Light debris is priced by truck volume. Heavy material — concrete, tile, dirt, roofing — is quoted by the cubic yard because weight, not space, drives the disposal cost. Recurring accounts get locked-in rates.
  • Firm quote before anything moves
  • Available in all 16 cities we serve
Call for a firm quote Text a photo

A dumpster is only cheaper if it is full and nobody else is filling it. On tight residential lots and occupied commercial buildings, a parked box costs you parking, permits, neighbour complaints and the volume that strangers put in it overnight. We haul on your schedule instead: framing scrap after rough-in, drywall after hanging, tile and packaging after finish, and a final sweep before the walkthrough.

Everything is priced by volume or by the cubic yard for heavy material, quoted before we load, and invoiced to your company. Clean wood, concrete, metal and cardboard get separated so your disposal cost drops and your diversion numbers hold up if a job requires documentation. For multi-phase projects we set a recurring visit so you stop thinking about debris altogether.

Construction Site Clean-Up questions

Is this cheaper than renting a dumpster?
It depends on how fast you fill it. For a steady multi-week build with high volume, a roll-off usually wins. For tight lots, occupied buildings, phase clean-ups and anything where a box would sit half-empty or attract outside dumping, hauling on demand is normally cheaper and always less hassle. We will tell you which one your job actually is.
How fast can you get to an active site?
Same-day or next-day across most of Contra Costa and Solano County. Established contractor accounts get priority dispatch, which in practice means a morning call is usually cleared that afternoon.
Can you handle concrete, dirt and other heavy material?
Yes, quoted by the cubic yard. Heavy material is limited by truck weight rather than bed volume, so we bring the right configuration and may split it across loads. Tell us what it is on the phone and the quote will be accurate.
